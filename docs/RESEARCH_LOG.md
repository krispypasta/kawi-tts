## 3. Phonetic Reconstruction Notes

### Working Hypotheses vs. Established Facts

| Feature | Established Status | Working Hypothesis | Evidence / Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pepet (*ě*) | Established central vowel | Mid-central vowel /ə/ | Universal in Javanese historical phonology. |
| Long Vowels (*ā, ī, ū*) | Metric evidence in Kakawin | Distinct duration /aː, iː, uː/ in formal recitation; quality distinction possible | Zoetmulder (1974). |
| Retroflex Stops (*ṭ, ḍ*) | Distinct in script & loanwords | True retroflex [ʈ, ɖ] vs dental [t̪, d̪] or alveolar | Sanskrit loanword phonology vs native retroflex. |
| Aspirated Stops (*kh, gh, th, dh, ph, bh*) | Attested in orthography for Sanskrit loans | Realized as aspirated in scholastic reading; neutralized in colloquial speech | Hunter; comparative Austronesian. |
